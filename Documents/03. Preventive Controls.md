# 03. Preventive Controls


My recommendations focus on **preventing these issues from recurring**, not just fixing the accounts identified.

1. **Leavers**  
   Link HR termination dates to automatic account deactivation and access removal. Verify completion and flag any access left active.

2. **Contractors**  
   Set access to end with the approved contract period. Require a documented extension before allowing access to continue.

3. **Privileged Access**  
   Require business justification and approval for Admin access. Conduct quarterly access recertification with the application owner and remove privileges no longer needed.

4. **Shared Accounts**  
   Replace shared logins with named accounts so activity can be traced to individuals. Confirm staff have the access they need before disabling the shared account.

5. **Orphaned and Dormant Accounts**  
   Keep an account inventory with named owners and business purposes. Flag accounts with no owner or prolonged inactivity, and review them for deactivation.

6. **Extended Leave**  
   Have HR and IAM review whether access should be retained, reduced or suspended during leave. Reassess it when the employee returns.
