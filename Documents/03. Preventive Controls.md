## 03. Preventive Controls

My recommendations focus on **preventing** these issues from recurring, not just fixing the accounts identified.

- **Leavers:** Link HR termination dates to automatic account deactivation and access removal. Verify completion and flag any access left active.


- **Contractors:** Set access to end with the approved contract period. Require a documented extension before allowing access to continue.


- **Privileged Access:** Require business justification and approval for Admin access. Conduct quarterly access recertification with the application owner and remove privileges no longer needed.


- **Shared Accounts:** Replace shared logins with named accounts so activity can be traced to individuals. Confirm staff have the access they need before disabling the shared account.


- **Orphaned and Dormant Accounts:** Keep an account inventory with named owners and business purposes. Flag accounts with no owner or prolonged inactivity, and review them for deactivation.


- **Extended Leave:** Have HR and IAM review whether access should be retained, reduced or suspended during leave. Reassess it when the employee returns.
