## 04. Summary for the IT Manager

**Key findings:** Two accounts had recorded logins after their employment or contract end dates, one with Admin access. The review also found an orphaned Admin account, shared credentials and a dormant account. Patient data may be at risk, but the records do not prove a breach.

**Immediate actions:** Verify Sarah Kim's account status, disable if still active and investigate the login. Confirm whether Tom Reyes has an approved contract extension; if not, disable his access and investigate.

**Recommendations:** Establish an HR-to-IT offboarding process, enforce contractor expiry dates, assign account owners, replace shared logins and conduct regular access reviews.
